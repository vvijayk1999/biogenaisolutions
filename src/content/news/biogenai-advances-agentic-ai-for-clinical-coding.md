## Transforming Clinical Information into Intelligent Workflows

BioGenAI Solutions is advancing the application of **Agentic AI and multi-agent architectures** to complex healthcare workflows, including clinical information extraction and medical coding.

The approach combines specialized AI components for extracting clinical information, retrieving relevant knowledge, evaluating potential recommendations, and validating results before presenting information for human review.

## From Clinical Information to AI-Assisted Decisions

A representative architecture can follow:

**Clinical Information → Extraction Agent → Knowledge Retrieval → AI Recommendation → Validation Agent → Human Review**

Rather than depending on a single Large Language Model, this architecture assigns specialized responsibilities to different AI components.
